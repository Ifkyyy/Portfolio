About

Who is Ifk?

I am a professional software developer who dabble in coding for fandom on the side. I have created several site skins and user scripts for the Archive of Our Own.

UX, design, and accessibility is always a priority in all my work.

I am also a hobbyist writer, and have several works on Archive of Our Own, and Fanfiction.net. I only write for a handful of fandoms, but you might also catch me in the comment sections of several other fandoms.
